Cacio e Pepe

Description

This cacio e pepe recipe makes for an easy, and efficient lunch.

Ingredients

  • 200g bucatini or spaghetti
  • 25g butter
  • 2 tsp whole black peppercorns, ground
  • 50g pecorino or parmesan, finely grated

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water for 2 minutes less than stated on the packet.
  2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a medium frying pan over a low heat, then add your ground black pepper, and toast for a few minutes.
  3. Drain the pasta, keeping 200ml of the pasta water.
  4. Tip the pasta along with 100ml of pasta water into the pan with the butter and pepper.
  5. Toss briefly, then sprinkle the pecorino/parmesan evenly, waiting 30 seconds to let it melt before stirring.
  6. Add a splash of pasta water and serve with a grating of black pepper.
