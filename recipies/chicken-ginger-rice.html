Chicken Ginger Rice

Description

Ingredients

  • 2 cups jasmine rice
  • 2 1/2 cups chicken stock
  • 3 slices fresh ginger

Steps

  1. Wash the rice to remove excess starch
  2. Place the rice into a saucepan, adding the chicken stock and nestling the ginger into the rice. Heat on high heat until the stock simmers, then reduce to medium heat and cook till rice grains begin to rise above the stock.
  3. Cover with lid that has a gap to let some steam escape, cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Confirm rice has absorbed all the chicken stock, then let the pot rest for 10 minutes.
  5. Fluff rice using fork and remove ginger.
  6. Serve!
