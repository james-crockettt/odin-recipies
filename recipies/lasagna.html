Lasagna

Description

This recipe is designed to be simple, and quick for you to make! Making use of store bought sauce to reduce the time spent prepping and cooking.

This lasagna won't demand that you carve out a massive block of time, and thats the best part about it.

Ingredients

  • 500g of Beef Mince
  • 680g of Marinara Sauce
  • Shredded Mozarella Cheese
  • Whole-milk Ricotta
  • Lasagna Noodles

Steps

  1. Brown the beef - Cook the beef in a pan, breaking the beef into small pieces using a wooden spoon until cooked through.
  2. Prepare the baking dish - Spread a thin layer of marinara sauce on the bottom of the dish, and mix the rest in with the cooked beef mince.
  3. Layer the lasagna - Place lasagna noodles in a layer in the baking dish, breaking if needed to keep it as a single layer. Spread 1 cup of ricotta cheese over the noodles. Then spread the beef and sauce mixture, topping with mozzarella.
  4. Repeat step 3 until all ingredients are used
  5. Bake- Bake the lasagna for 1 hour, until the cheese on top is melted and the noodles are done.
  6. Let rest and then serve!
